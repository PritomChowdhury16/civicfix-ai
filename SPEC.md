# CivicFix AI

## 1. Project Overview

CivicFix AI is an AI-powered platform for reporting and managing local environmental and civic problems.

The first version will focus on waste-related problems. Users can submit a report with a description, photo, and location. The platform will organize these reports so that responsible people can review and manage them.

In future versions, the platform can be expanded to support problems such as potholes, waterlogging, broken streetlights, and other local civic issues.

## 2. Problem

Local environmental problems such as garbage dumping and unmanaged waste are often difficult to report, track, and manage efficiently.

Citizens may notice a problem but may not know where to report it or whether the issue is being addressed.

CivicFix AI aims to provide a simple digital platform where people can report problems and track their status.

## 3. Target Users

* Local residents
* Students
* Community volunteers
* Local organizations
* Problem-management teams or administrators

## 4. Core Features

### Report a Problem

Users can submit:

* Problem description
* Photo
* Location
* Problem category

### Reports

Users can view submitted reports and their current status.

### Report Details

Each report will have a dedicated page showing its details, priority, location, and status.

### Dashboard

An administrative dashboard will provide an overview of reported problems.

### AI Analysis

In later stages, AI will analyze submitted reports and help identify:

* Problem category
* Severity
* Priority
* Possible responsible department or organization

## 5. Initial Problem Category
